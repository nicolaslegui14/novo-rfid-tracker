Designed in California. Named for its coast.

Cabrillo started with a simple complaint: good leather shoes cost too much at the department store, and cheaper ones look tired within a season.

We design a short collection of shoes, belts and bags meant to get better with use. The leather darkens, softens and creases to your shape. The point is the pair you have in five years, not the pair in the box.

The collection is designed in California. We're choosing our workshop now, and we'll name it here, with photos, once the first run is made.
